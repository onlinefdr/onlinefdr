A few questions before your first session

The call you make before you call a lawyer.

We ask everyone these questions before they start. It helps the practitioner make sure the process is safe and fair for you, and that you have the right support around you.
  • There are no right or wrong answers — answer what’s true for you.
  • If you’d rather not answer something here, you can leave it and talk it through in your session.
  • Fill it in somewhere private, on your own, where you won’t be overheard.
  • It takes about five minutes.
