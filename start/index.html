What the discovery call covers

A short, no-obligation conversation with an accredited FDR practitioner. Nothing is decided on the call.

  • Your situation, heardYou explain what is happening. The practitioner listens and asks a few questions to understand where things stand.
  • How the process works for youA plain-language walk through of how FDR runs for your circumstances, whether your matter involves children, property, or both.
  • Whether FDR is right for youAn honest read on whether Family Dispute Resolution suits your matter. If it is not the right path, the practitioner will say so and point you in the right direction.

No obligation. The practitioner assigned to your matter does not take sides and does not decide the outcome. They help each person shape and reality-check the proposals they put to each other.
